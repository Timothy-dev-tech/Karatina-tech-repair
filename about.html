About Us

Karatina Tech & Repair started with a simple problem: getting a phone fixed in Karatina usually meant traveling to Nyeri or Nairobi, waiting days, and hoping the part was genuine.

We set out to change that — a repair shop based right in town, with same-day turnaround on most common issues and parts we’d trust in our own phones.

Every repair comes with a 90-day warranty, not because it’s required, but because we stand behind the work. Whether it’s a cracked screen, a dying battery, or a phone that took an unfortunate trip through the rain, our goal is the same: get you back to full performance, without the runaround.
